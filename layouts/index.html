Welcome to JudaicaLink!

JudaicaLink is the knowledge graph behind the Specialized Information Service Jewish Studies (Fachinformationsdienst Jüdische Studien, FID) of the University Library Johann Christian Senckenberg in Frankfurt am Main. It brings together reference works, encyclopediae and research data on Jewish culture and history as Linked Open Data.

This is the site of the JudaicaLink project at Mannheim Technical University, where JudaicaLink is developed further: the data model, the generation and cleaning of the data, the entity pages and new ways to use the graph. Here you find news about our work, the datasets of our current data version, our ontology, the source code and the people behind the project.

New developments are shown on mannheim.judaicalink.org before they go into production, with the latest version of the knowledge graph and these showcases:

  • Atlas: the people, organisations and publications of JudaicaLink on a map, in time and space, with their connections.
  • Footprints: where Jewish books were printed and where their copies went.
  • Compact Memory: the Jewish periodicals, by full text and by the people, places and works they mention.
  • Library of Haskala: the books, authors and printers of the Jewish Enlightenment.

We are always looking for datasets that extend the knowledge graph. If you are responsible for a reference work or a research dataset on Jewish Studies, please get in touch.

JudaicaLink in Frankfurt

The JudaicaLink knowledge graph is operated as part of the portal of the FID Jewish Studies at Goethe University Library Frankfurt. Search the graph, browse the datasets and use the interfaces there.
