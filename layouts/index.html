Welcome to JudaicaLink!

JudaicaLink is in production in Frankfurt

The JudaicaLink knowledge graph is operated as part of the portal of the Specialized Information Service Jewish Studies (FID Jüdische Studien) at Goethe University Library Frankfurt. Search the graph, browse the datasets and use the interfaces there.

This site

This is the site of the JudaicaLink project at Mannheim Technical University, where JudaicaLink is developed further. Research and development happen here; the service itself runs in Frankfurt. You will find news about ongoing work, our ontology, the source code and the people behind the project.

Jewish Studies

JudaicaLink is the knowledge graph behind the specialized information service for Jewish Studies (Fachinformationsdienst Jüdische Studien, FID) of the University Library Johann Christian Senckenberg in Frankfurt am Main. It brings together reference works, encyclopediae and research data on Jewish culture and history as Linked Open Data.

Contribute data

We are always looking for datasets that extend the knowledge graph. If you are responsible for a reference work or a research dataset on Jewish Studies, please get in touch – the team page lists who to contact in Frankfurt and in Mannheim.
