JudaicaLink in Frankfurt

The JudaicaLink knowledge graph is operated as part of the portal of the Specialized Information Service Jewish Studies (FID Jüdische Studien) at Goethe University Library Frankfurt. Search the graph, browse the datasets and use the interfaces there.

This site

This is the site of the JudaicaLink project at Mannheim Technical University, where JudaicaLink is developed further. We use the site to demonstrate new developments before they go into production. You will find news about ongoing work, our ontology, the source code and the people behind the project.

Our demo server mannheim.judaicalink.org shows the latest changes to the knowledge graph, with two showcases: Compact Memory, access to the Jewish periodicals by full text and by the people, places and works they mention, and the Library of Haskala.

Jewish Studies

JudaicaLink is the knowledge graph behind the specialized information service for Jewish Studies (Fachinformationsdienst Jüdische Studien, FID) of the University Library Johann Christian Senckenberg in Frankfurt am Main. It brings together reference works, encyclopediae and research data on Jewish culture and history as Linked Open Data.

Contribute data

We are always looking for datasets that extend the knowledge graph. If you are responsible for a reference work or a research dataset on Jewish Studies, please get in touch.
