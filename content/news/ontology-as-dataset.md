The vocabulary that describes the knowledge graph is now available in the same way as the data it
describes.
<!--more-->

JudaicaLink has its own ontology, built on SKOS and aligned with the GND. Until now it was
documented separately; publishing it as a dataset means it can be retrieved, queried and reused
through the same interfaces as everything else. For anyone working with our RDF, this makes it
easier to find out what a property actually means.

[Dataset description](https://www.jewishstudies.de/en/judaicalink/datasets/dataset/ontology/) · [Ontology documentation](https://ontology.judaicalink.org/judaicalink-ontology)
