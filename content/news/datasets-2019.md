Over the past year, five datasets have been added to JudaicaLink: authors from two libraries, a bibliography of
Jewish education, and two regional datasets on persons.

<!--more-->

- [Persons from the National Library of Israel](/datasets/nli-persons/): the authors in the authority file of the
  NLI, with dates of birth and death and their publications where available.
- [Persons from the University Library Frankfurt](/datasets/ubffm-persons/): the authors of the Judaica collection of
  the University Library Johann Christian Senckenberg, enriched with data from the GND where a GND number is known.
- [Resources on German-Jewish Education and Religion](/datasets/jre/): bibliographic information on texts used in
  Jewish education and religious practice in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, from bible editions and
  sermons to textbooks, with information on their authors. The database comes from the research project
  "Innovation through Tradition?" of the Georg Eckert Institute, the German Historical Institute Washington and
  Tel Aviv University.
- [Stolpersteine in Mainz](/datasets/stolpersteine/): the stumbling blocks laid in Mainz since 2007 in memory of
  people persecuted during the National Socialist era, with the persons they commemorate.
- [A Jewish family from Aschaffenburg](/datasets/hirsch-family/): the Hirsch family, researched by the association
  Jüdisches Leben in Unterfranken – Biographische Datenbank e.V.

All five are published under CC0.
