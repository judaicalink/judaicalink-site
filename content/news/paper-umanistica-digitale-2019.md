Our paper on JudaicaLink has appeared in the open access journal *Umanistica Digitale*.

<!--more-->

The paper describes how JudaicaLink is built and how it can be used: its sources, from encyclopedias such as Das
Jüdische Hamburg to general knowledge bases such as the GND, its architecture and technical implementation, and its
applications, in particular the contextualisation of library metadata through entity resolution and linking.

Kai Eckert, Maral Dadvar: JudaicaLink: A Knowledge Base for Jewish Culture and History. In: *Umanistica Digitale*
3 (4), 2019. <https://doi.org/10.6092/issn.2532-8816/9047>
