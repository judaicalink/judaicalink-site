The documentation of JudaicaLink is now online at [judaicalink-docs.readthedocs.io](https://judaicalink-docs.readthedocs.io/),
in English and German.

<!--more-->

It explains how to use JudaicaLink, from searching and content negotiation to the datasets and SPARQL, and documents
the software behind it for developers: the web application, the generators, the Linked Data frontend, DjangoRDF and
the RDF generator, with guides to setup, deployment and Docker. The sources are on
[GitHub](https://github.com/judaicalink/judaicalink-docs).
