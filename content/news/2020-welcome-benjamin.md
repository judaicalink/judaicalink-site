Benjamin Schnabel has joined the JudaicaLink team as web developer and researcher.

<!--more-->

Benjamin works on JudaicaLink Labs, the web application in which the data of JudaicaLink can be searched and
explored, and on the processing of Compact Memory, the full-text collection of Jewish periodicals of the University
Library Frankfurt.
