A regional bibliography with some 7,000 references has been added to the knowledge graph.
<!--more-->

Regional bibliographies are hard to find and easy to overlook, which is precisely why they are
worth linking. The Bibliografie zur deutsch-jüdischen Geschichte Nordrhein-Westfalens documents
research literature on the history and culture of Jews in the region. Through JudaicaLink its
references become reachable from the persons and places they concern.

[Dataset description](https://www.jewishstudies.de/en/judaicalink/datasets/dataset/nrwbibdjg/)
