With the new year, the Specialized Information Service (FID) Jewish Studies has started at the University Library
Johann Christian Senckenberg in Frankfurt am Main, funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG). JudaicaLink is part
of it.

<!--more-->

The FID provides researchers in Germany working in Jewish Studies and Israel Studies with specialised information,
resources and services. It is run by the University Library Frankfurt together with Stuttgart Media University, where
JudaicaLink is developed.

Within the FID, JudaicaLink links the data of the library with external resources: encyclopedias, authority files and
research databases. This way, the metadata of the FID can be enriched, and persons, places and subjects can be found
across sources. The first funding phase runs until 2018.
