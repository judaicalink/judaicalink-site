The FID Jewish Studies and the academy project Buber-Korrespondenzen Digital held a workshop on
the potential of authority data, on 30 September and 1 October 2024 at Goethe University
Frankfurt.
<!--more-->

Around 25 researchers and library professionals took part. The programme covered data services
for research, the GND within the National Research Data Infrastructure, and Buber-Korrespondenzen
Digital as a worked example, alongside hands-on sessions on data curation and a tour of the
Hebraica and Judaica collections.

For JudaicaLink the workshop was more than a presentation opportunity. The wish to work with
openly curated, collaboratively maintained data, expressed clearly by the participants, is one of
the reasons why the integration with Wikidata has become a focus of our current work.
