Benjamin Schnabel has published a final report on extending the Compact Memory Judaica with
titles held by the German National Library.
<!--more-->

Compact Memory, the digital collection of historical Jewish periodicals at Frankfurt University
Library, is one of the sources JudaicaLink draws on: entities extracted from its full texts are
linked into the knowledge graph, which is what makes the Compact Memory entity search possible.
The report documents how the underlying set of titles was extended and what that required in
terms of data work.

Benjamin Schnabel: Ergänzung der Judaica von Compact Memory mit Titeln aus der Deutschen
Nationalbibliothek. Abschlussbericht. Leipzig, Frankfurt am Main 2024, ISSN 2944-0904.
