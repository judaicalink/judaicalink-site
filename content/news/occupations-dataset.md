Occupations that previously existed only as free text in the graph are now available as a
controlled set of concepts.
<!--more-->

Our sources describe what people did for a living in many different ways, in several languages
and spellings. As long as those descriptions remain plain literals, they cannot be searched or
compared reliably. The Occupations dataset gathers the occupation values used across JudaicaLink
and turns them into reusable concepts, which is a precondition for faceting by occupation and for
linking to external vocabularies.

[Dataset description](https://www.jewishstudies.de/en/judaicalink/datasets/dataset/occupations/)
