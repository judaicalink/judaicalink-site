Maral Dadvar has joined the JudaicaLink team as a postdoctoral researcher in the project FID Jewish Studies.

<!--more-->

Maral is an expert in natural language processing, with a PhD in Human Media Interaction from the University of
Twente and an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Sheffield. She works at the University Library
Johann Christian Senckenberg in Frankfurt am Main, where she builds up JudaicaLink as a knowledge base for Jewish
culture and history, to be used in the FID to enrich the library's data.
