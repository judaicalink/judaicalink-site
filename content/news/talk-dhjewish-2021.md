At the conference #DHJewish – Jewish Studies and Digital Humanities, hosted by the C²DH of the University of
Luxembourg, we showed how the JudaicaLink knowledge graph opens up the full text of Compact Memory.

<!--more-->

Compact Memory, the collection of Jewish periodicals of the University Library Frankfurt, can be searched by its
full text. Linking the persons and places mentioned in it to JudaicaLink lets researchers find pages by what the
knowledge graph knows about them, for example pages mentioning rabbis, or persons born in a certain city.

Benjamin Schnabel, Kai Eckert: Using a Knowledge Graph to explore Full Text Databases. #DHJewish – Jewish Studies and
Digital Humanities, C²DH, University of Luxembourg, January 2021.
