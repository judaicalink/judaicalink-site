At the panel on entity management of the DCMI Virtual Conference 2021, we presented how JudaicaLink manages the
entities it brings together from many sources.

<!--more-->

JudaicaLink collects and integrates data on persons, places and other entities from encyclopedias, library data and
authority files. The talk showed how records about the same entity are linked and joined, and what we learned: link
early, to avoid having to merge entities later, but avoid wrong links from the start, as they lead to clusters that
have to be split again. The panel was moderated by Jian Qin, with talks by Antoine Isaac, Cuijuan Xia, Karen F. Gracy
and Katherine Louise Polley.
