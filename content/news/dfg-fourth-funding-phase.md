The German Research Foundation has approved a new three-year funding phase for the FID Jüdische
Studien, with up to two million euros for the central research information infrastructure for
Jewish and Israel Studies.
<!--more-->

Mannheim Technical University is a partner in the project alongside the University Library Johann
Christian Senckenberg in Frankfurt am Main and the State and University Library Hamburg. In
Mannheim, the work continues on JudaicaLink, the Linked Open Data service that connects and
curates specialised data resources for Jewish Studies through a knowledge graph and curated
authority data. JudaicaLink is integrated into the information portal of the FID.

The funding period runs from 2026 to 2028. For JudaicaLink the focus lies on consolidating the
service for long-term operation and on opening it up further, in particular towards Wikidata, so
that researchers can contribute to the data themselves.
