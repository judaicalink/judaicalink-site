An article in *Bibliotheksdienst* describes the role of JudaicaLink in the Specialized Information Service (FID)
Jewish Studies.

<!--more-->

The article (in German) introduces the FID Jewish Studies at the University Library Frankfurt and its portal, and
shows how JudaicaLink prepares and enriches the metadata of the FID by linking it to external resources.

Kai Eckert, Rachel Heuberger, Marko Knepper: JudaicaLink und der FID Jüdische Studien. In: *Bibliotheksdienst*
52 (3–4), 2018, pp. 266–277. <https://doi.org/10.1515/bd-2018-0032>

*Update 2023: an open access copy is available in the
[repository of the University of Mainz](https://openscience.ub.uni-mainz.de/handle/20.500.12030/9094).*
