Since JudaicaLink moved into production in Frankfurt, our former labs environment is no longer needed
for the service. Its place is taken by [mannheim.judaicalink.org](https://mannheim.judaicalink.org/),
the new home for showcasing our work in Mannheim.
<!--more-->

The site runs our current version of the knowledge graph, including the latest changes to the data
before they reach production: a [SPARQL endpoint](https://mannheim.judaicalink.org/sparql/), a view of
every JudaicaLink resource, a [graph search](https://mannheim.judaicalink.org/search/), a
[dashboard](https://mannheim.judaicalink.org/dashboard/) and the
[dumps](https://mannheim.judaicalink.org/dumps/) of the loaded versions.

It starts with two showcases:

- [Compact Memory](https://mannheim.judaicalink.org/compact-memory/): full-text search over the
  Jewish periodicals of Compact Memory, and search by the people, places and works they mention.
- [Library of Haskala](https://mannheim.judaicalink.org/haskala/): the books, authors and printers of
  the Jewish Enlightenment, as collected in the Library of Haskala (2004–2015).

The JudaicaLink service itself, with search, SPARQL and the REST API, is part of the
[FID Jewish Studies portal](https://www.jewishstudies.de/en/judaicalink/).
