The online source edition of the Institute for the History of German Jews has been added to the
knowledge graph.
<!--more-->

The Hamburger Schlüsseldokumente present key documents on German-Jewish history, each accompanied
by scholarly commentary. For JudaicaLink, the interesting part is the persons, places and events
described around those documents: once linked, they connect the edition to the other sources in
the graph, so that a person known from an encyclopedia entry can also be found in a commented
source.

[Dataset description](https://www.jewishstudies.de/en/judaicalink/datasets/dataset/hhkeydocs/)
