Footprints traces the movement of Jewish books since the beginning of print. The dataset is now
part of JudaicaLink.
<!--more-->

Footprints records where individual copies of a book have been over time: who owned them, where
they surfaced, how they travelled. That makes it a different kind of source from the reference
works in the graph, which describe persons and concepts. Linking the two means that provenance
information and biographical information can be followed in one place. For JudaicaLink the
dataset was supplemented with work titles.

[Dataset description](https://www.jewishstudies.de/en/judaicalink/datasets/dataset/footprints/)
