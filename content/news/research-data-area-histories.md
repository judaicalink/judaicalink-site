Kerstin von der Krone describes the research data work of the FID Jewish Studies and its role in
NFDI4Memory.
<!--more-->

The article sets out how the FID acts as mediator, expert and data provider, and where
JudaicaLink fits into that: as a background service that prepares subject-specific data as Linked
Open Data and enriches the metadata of the FID. It is a useful piece for anyone wanting to
understand the wider infrastructure JudaicaLink is part of, rather than the service in isolation.

Kerstin von der Krone: Forschungsdaten in den Area Histories: der Fachinformationsdienst Jüdische
Studien und die NFDI4Memory. In: Connections (2025).
<https://doi.org/10.60693/ec5w-ek22>
