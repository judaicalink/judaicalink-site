A paper on the method behind provenance in JudaicaLink has appeared in *The Electronic Library*.
<!--more-->

JudaicaLink aggregates data from many sources. For a knowledge graph built this way, it matters
not only what is stated about an entity, but also where each statement comes from. Keeping that
information attached to the individual statement, rather than to the dataset as a whole, is what
allows us to show the source next to every property on an entity page.

The paper describes how this can be modelled with RDF-star, and what it means for the data
workflows around it. It builds on earlier work presented at KGSWC 2022 and provides the basis
for taking in data curated elsewhere without losing track of its origin.

Florian Rupp, Benjamin Schnabel, Kai Eckert: Implementing Data Workflows and Data Model
Extensions with RDF-star. In: The Electronic Library 42.3 (2024), 393–412.
<https://doi.org/10.1108/EL-04-2023-0102>
