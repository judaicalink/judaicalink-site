At iiWAS 2019 in Munich, we presented a method to match names in Hebrew script with their transliterations in Latin
script.

<!--more-->

Library records and reference works often give the names of persons in Latin transliteration, while other sources
use the Hebrew original, and the transliteration rules differ between them. Transliterating back into Hebrew script
gives a common form in which records about the same person can be found and linked, a step towards the
disambiguation of entities in JudaicaLink.

Aaron Christianson, Maral Dadvar, Kai Eckert: Reverse-Transliteration of Hebrew script for Entity Disambiguation.
In: *Proceedings of the 21st International Conference on Information Integration and Web-based Applications &
Services (iiWAS 2019)*, Munich, 2019, pp. 335–338. <https://doi.org/10.1145/3366030.3366099>
