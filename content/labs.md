# JudaicaLink Labs

**If a link brought you here, the service you were looking for has moved.**

JudaicaLink Labs (`labs.judaicalink.org`) was the prototype system developed in Mannheim.
It served as the blueprint for the production implementation that now runs in the portal of
the Specialized Information Service Jewish Studies at Goethe University Library Frankfurt.
The background of the move is described under [JudaicaLink in production](/production/).

## What happens to Labs

Labs is being retired. Requests to `labs.judaicalink.org` are redirected to this page for
the time being. Whether Mannheim will again host demonstrators and experimental work under a
separate address is open; if so, it will be announced in the [news](/news/).
